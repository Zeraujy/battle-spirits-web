# Online Validation

Online validation is performed by executable audits and regression tests, not historical phase documents.

Coverage includes:

- online domain foundation;
- authoritative synchronization;
- casual matchmaking;
- ready checks;
- pre-match deck lock and VS handoff;
- ranked matchmaking and result authority;
- social match flows;
- match history and public error UX;
- online security and sanitization;
- full multiplayer and ranked regression suites.

The automated tests under `src/online/` and `server/` are the canonical behavioral reference.
